# PRD Generator
ID: PE-001 | Version: 1.0.0 | Category: software-engineering

Generate a comprehensive Product Requirements Document covering problem statement, users, goals, requirements, constraints, and success metrics.

================================================================================

Act as a Senior Product Manager and Technical Architect.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive Product Requirements Document (PRD) for:

PRODUCT: {product_name}
OVERVIEW: {product_overview}

The PRD must include:

## 1. Problem Statement
- What problem does this solve?
- Who experiences this problem?
- Current alternatives and their limitations

## 2. Target Users
- Primary user personas
- User needs and pain points
- Usage context and environment

## 3. Goals and Non-Goals
- What this product WILL accomplish
- What this product will NOT do (explicit exclusions)
- Success criteria

## 4. User Stories
- Format: As a [user], I want [capability] so that [benefit]
- Cover critical user journeys
- Include edge cases and error scenarios

## 5. Functional Requirements
- Core features with detailed specifications
- User interactions and workflows
- Data requirements
- Integration points

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements
- Performance targets (latency, throughput)
- Scalability requirements
- Security and privacy requirements
- Accessibility requirements (WCAG 2.1 AA minimum)
- Browser/platform compatibility

## 7. Technical Constraints
- Technology stack limitations
- Infrastructure constraints
- Budget constraints
- Timeline constraints
- Compliance requirements

## 8. Success Metrics
- Quantitative KPIs
- Qualitative measures
- Measurement methodology

## 9. Risks and Mitigation
- Technical risks
- Business risks
- User adoption risks
- Mitigation strategies

## 10. Acceptance Criteria
- Feature completeness checklist
- Quality gates
- Launch readiness criteria

FORMAT: Structured Markdown with clear headers and bullet points.

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:
- Be specific and measurable
- Avoid vague requirements like "fast" or "user-friendly"
- Include concrete examples where helpful
- Distinguish must-haves from nice-to-haves
- Call out assumptions explicitly


REQUIRED INPUTS:

- product_name (string, REQUIRED): Name of the product or feature
- product_overview (string, REQUIRED): Brief overview of what the product does


QUALITY CHECKS:

- Problem statement is clear and specific
- User personas are well-defined
- Requirements are measurable and testable
- Non-goals prevent scope creep
- Success metrics are quantifiable
- Risks have mitigation strategies
